Outcome Language Checker

Paste one outcome, or a full set with one outcome per line. The checker reads the wording and points out the patterns that most often send an outcome back for revision. Nothing you paste leaves this page.

What this is, and what it is not. This is a first pass on wording only. It cannot tell whether an outcome fits your discipline, your course, or the assessments you have planned. Those are the questions that matter most, and they are the ones worth a conversation with TLDE.

One outcome per line. Numbering and a lead-in line such as "Upon completion, students will be able to:" are fine to include.

The guidance behind each note comes from the TLDE Outcomes pages, which draw on Anderson and Krathwohl's revised Bloom taxonomy (2001), the HLC Criteria for Accreditation (Criteria 3.A and 3.E), the SUNY OSCQR rubric, and the OLC QCTIP framework. No accreditor publishes a list of forbidden verbs; the notes here are recommendations, not rules.
